Our Story

From an Heirloom Orchard to a Regenerative Movement

In 1948, Samuel Ward returned from the Pacific and acquired a modest 40-acre parcel in Riverdale Valley. Armed with antique hand plows and heirloom apple saplings, he made a solemn vow: to leave the soil richer, darker, and more vibrant than he found it.

When industrial agriculture pushed synthetic fertilizers and chemical monocropping in the 1970s, the Ward family deliberately refused. Instead, they doubled down on cover crops, earthworm composting, and heirloom seed preservation.

Today, Verdant Heritage spans 350 certified organic acres. Under the leadership of 4th-generation farmer Thomas Ward, we combine ancient biological wisdom with modern solar energy and cold-chain logistics to feed over 18,500 households.

Ecological Stewardship

Our 4 Core Regenerative Pillars

We farm to regenerate ecosystems, sequester atmospheric carbon, and elevate nutrient density.

🪱

Zero-Till Living Soil Biology

Tillage destroys fragile mycorrhizal fungal networks. By leaving soil undisturbed and using roller-crimped cover crops, our topsoil retains immense moisture and naturally stores tons of carbon per acre.

🐝

Pollinator Corridors & Biodiversity

Over 40 acres of our land are dedicated exclusively to native wildflower meadows, hedgerows, and honeybee apiaries. Beneficial ladybugs and parasitic wasps provide 100% natural pest control.

💧

Solar-Powered Drip Irrigation

Our well water is drawn via rooftop solar arrays and delivered directly to plant root zones through micro-drip emitters, cutting water usage by 65% compared to industrial spray overhead pivots.

♻️

Closed-Loop Compost & Biochar

All crop trimmings, bakery chaff, and pastured hen bedding are thermally composted on-site. We infuse it with biochar to create dark, fragrant humus that enriches next season's seedbeds.
