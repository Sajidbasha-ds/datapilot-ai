Visitor Guidelines & Tips

  • 👟 Footwear: Wear comfortable closed-toe boots or walking shoes suitable for unpaved farm trails.
  • 👒 Sun Protection: We recommend wide-brim hats, sunscreen, and refillable water bottles.
  • 🐕 Pets: For the safety of our pastured heritage hens, pets are not permitted on working field plots (service animals welcome).
  • 🌦️ Weather: Tours run rain or shine! In the event of heavy rain, tours move into our historic glass greenhouses.
