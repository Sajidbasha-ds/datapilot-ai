Generations of Stewardship

Rooted in Passion. Growing for the Future.

For four generations, the Ward family has cultivated these 350 rolling acres with deep reverence for natural ecological cycles. What began as an heirloom fruit orchard has blossomed into a modern regenerative sanctuary.

We believe real flavor comes from living, biodiverse soil teeming with earthworms and beneficial microbes. We cultivate over 120 varieties of heirloom plants, harness 100% solar power, and operate our own closed-loop compost system.
